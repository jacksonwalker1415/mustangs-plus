About this project

SMU+ was built for an SMU Cox MBA class project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SMU, SMU Athletics, Mustang Partners, Learfield or any athlete or organization shown.

Memberships are for demonstration only. No payments are taken and no real product or service is offered. For educational purposes only.

Contact: smuplusmedia@gmail.com

Sources

Real schedules, results, rosters and coaches come from SMU Athletics. Player profiles list their own sources on each page, and sourced stories list theirs at the end. X posts come from each account’s own page.

    What we collect

    • Waitlist: your email address, your name, SMU affiliation and the plan you’d be interested in if you choose to give them, the date you joined, that you agreed to this notice, and how you found the site (the campaign tag in the link you used, such as a QR code, and the name of the website that linked you, if any).
    • Page visits: a simple visit count. For each page load we save the page address, the campaign tag and linking website (if any), whether you’re on a phone or a computer, and a random code that lasts only until you close the tab, so visits in one session can be counted once. No cookies and no browser details are saved with the visit. Our hosting and database providers (GitHub Pages and Supabase) may log IP addresses in their standard request logs.
    • Post reports: if you report a post on the boards, we save which post, the reason you picked, your optional note and your account, so moderators can review it. Only moderators see reports; the person you reported is not told who reported them.
    • SMU+ Weekly sign-up: your email address.
    • Accounts (if you create one): your email address, the display name you choose, your board posts, the players you save, how far you got in videos, and your demo membership tier. If you upload a profile photo, we store that photo (cropped to a small square) and show it to other members next to your name and posts. You can delete it anytime from your Account page, which removes it from our storage. Your password is handled by Supabase’s sign-in service and is never visible to us. You can also sign in with Microsoft (for SMU email accounts) when that option is shown: Microsoft confirms who you are and shares your email address and name with SMU+, and Microsoft’s own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
    • Student sign-up (if you use it): your @smu.edu email, your name and your class year. We don’t ask for a student ID. The Student plan is granted only after you confirm your @smu.edu email from the link we send.

    We don’t collect payment details. SMU+ itself doesn’t set cookies or use advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Content from other services (see below) may set that service’s cookies. The visit count above is our own and stays in the SMU+ database. Posts from X in the “From the feeds” rows load automatically from X when you scroll near them, using X’s own embed, and X may set its own cookies. Videos and podcasts from other services (YouTube and Apple Podcasts) load from those services only when you choose to play them; until then, no request goes to them. The one exception: video cards and video pages show the video’s preview picture, which loads from YouTube’s image server (i.ytimg.com). Once something loads, that service’s own privacy policy applies, and it may set its own cookies. News stories from other outlets open on their own websites. The Follow rows are links to each account on X. A few writer and outlet accounts (in the “Conference and beat coverage” row and the From Socials Following list) show their X profile picture, which loads from X’s image server (pbs.twimg.com); the other accounts load nothing from X until you tap one.

    Embedded posts and videos from X, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok load content from those companies, and they may set their own cookies. On the message boards, a link to one of those services shows a placeholder first, and nothing loads from that service until you press its Load button. On the From Socials page and in the “From the feeds” rows, only posts from X are embedded, and they load from X as you scroll near them, including the first few as soon as the page opens.

    Why we collect it

    Waitlist and newsletter addresses are used only to contact you about this student project. Account details are used only to run the account features you use, such as posting on the boards and keeping your saved players.

    Where it’s stored

    Sign-ups, accounts, posts and profile photos are stored in a database and file storage run by Supabase, the hosting service this project uses. Access rules on the database stop visitors from reading the waitlist or anyone else’s email address. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and it loads its fonts from Google Fonts and one code library from jsDelivr. Like most web hosts, those services may keep standard request logs such as IP addresses.

    Selling and sharing

    We never sell your information and we don’t share it with anyone for advertising. It stays with this project.

    Deleting your data

    To ask for your data to be deleted, email SMU+ from the address you signed up with at smuplusmedia@gmail.com. Your waitlist entry, newsletter sign-up, account, profile photo and posts will be removed. You can remove your profile photo yourself at any time with Remove photo on your Account page. You can also ask what we hold about you.

    Your browser

    The site saves a few settings in your browser’s local storage, such as whether you’ve seen the loading screen and what you’ve watched. In demo mode, everything you enter stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    Contact

    Questions about this notice or this project: smuplusmedia@gmail.com. SMU+ is not intended for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. See also the terms of use.
