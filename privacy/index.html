About this project

SMU+ is an unofficial student concept project made by Jackson Walker, an MBA student at the SMU Cox School of Business, for a class. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SMU, SMU Athletics, Learfield or any athlete.

No payments are taken. Membership plans and checkout on this site are a demo. The site never asks for a card, and choosing a plan only changes a setting on a demo account.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, your name and SMU affiliation if you choose to give them, the date you joined and that you agreed to this notice.
  • The Boulevard Brief sign-up: your email address.
  • Accounts (if you create one): your email address, the display name you choose, your board posts, the players you save, how far you got in videos, and your demo membership tier. Your password is handled by Supabase’s sign-in service and is never visible to us.

We don’t collect payment details, and we don’t use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we collect it

Waitlist and newsletter addresses are used only to contact you about this student project. Account details are used only to run the account features you use, such as posting on the boards and keeping your saved players.

Where it’s stored

Sign-ups, accounts and posts are stored in a database run by Supabase, the hosting service this project uses. Access rules on the database stop visitors from reading the waitlist or anyone else’s email address. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and it loads its fonts from Google Fonts and one code library from jsDelivr. Like most web hosts, those services may keep standard request logs such as IP addresses.

Selling and sharing

We never sell your information and we don’t share it with anyone for advertising. It stays with this project.

Deleting your data

Email Jackson Walker at Jpwalker@smu.edu from the address you signed up with and ask for your data to be deleted. Your waitlist entry, newsletter sign-up, account and posts will be removed. You can also ask what we hold about you.

Your browser

The site saves a few settings in your browser’s local storage, such as whether you’ve seen the loading screen and what you’ve watched. In demo mode, everything you enter stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.
