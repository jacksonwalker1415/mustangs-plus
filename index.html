FAQ

Good questions.

What is Mustangs+?

Mustangs+ is a (concept) official fan membership for SMU athletics. Members unlock exclusive video series, long-form stories, podcasts and game-week access from every Mustang sport, all in one place.

How does the NIL funding work?

40% of every membership goes into a pooled Student-Athlete NIL Fund. Athletes are paid from that fund for real NIL activities like appearing in content, hosting Q&As and fan events.

Payouts follow NCAA, state and university NIL rules, and the fund's totals are reported to members every quarter.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Memberships are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel in two taps and keep access through the end of your billing period.

Is any content free?

Some highlights and articles are free to everyone. Items marked with a lock are members-only.

Who runs it?

In this concept, Mustangs+ would be operated by Learfield in partnership with SMU Athletics, similar to other school-branded "plus" platforms. This site is a class mockup only.
