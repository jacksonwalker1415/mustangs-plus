    Good questions.

    What is SMU+?

    SMU+ is an unofficial student concept for a fan membership built around SMU athletics. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SMU. In the concept, SMU+ would add original series, long-form stories, podcasts and game-week access from every Mustang sport, all in one place (coming soon; today the library carries official SMU Athletics video and sourced stories, free to everyone).

    How would NIL work?

    In the proposed model, a share of membership revenue would support SMU Athletics’ official NIL and revenue-sharing programs, with exact terms still to be decided and athlete deals disclosed through the NIL Go clearinghouse.

    SMU Athletics would decide how that money is used under NCAA, state and university rules. SMU+ would not choose or pay individual athletes or teams.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Memberships are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel in two taps and keep access through the end of your billing period.

    Is any content free?

    Yes. Everything on the site is free to read and watch. Making an account is free too.

    What are the message boards?

    The Mustang Board is a community forum like other team sites have. Anyone can read every board. Sign in to post. The Paddock has practice reports and insider posts from SMU+ staff.

    Who runs it?

    Today SMU+ is an unofficial student concept project for an SMU Cox class, run by a student. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SMU, SMU Athletics or Learfield, and no payments are taken. The concept imagines a school-branded "plus" platform like those at other schools, which would need SMU’s official approval to exist. About this project
