Community rules

The message boards are for SMU fans to talk sports. A few rules keep them worth reading.

  1. Leave people’s families out of it

    No harassment of athletes, coaches or their families. Criticize a play call or a lineup all you want. Don’t go after the person, and never their family.

  2. Recruits and minors are off limits

    No rumors or personal info about recruits or anyone under 18. Stick to what’s been announced publicly. Don’t guess at where a recruit is visiting, post their social accounts or contact them.

  3. No doxxing

    Don’t post anyone’s address, phone number, class schedule, workplace, car or photos of their home. That goes for fans, too.

  4. No spam

    No ads, scams, referral links or the same post over and over.

  5. Link, don’t copy

    Don’t copy whole threads to other sites. Link to the thread instead.

What moderators do

Mods may remove posts that break these rules, lock threads that go sideways, and suspend accounts that keep at it. When we remove something, we’ll try to tell you why.

See something?

Tap Report under any post, pick a reason, and a moderator will take a look. Only moderators see reports, and the poster isn’t told who reported them. To appeal a removal or reach the SMU+ team, email smuplusmedia@gmail.com.

SMU+ is a concept built for a class project. Real members post on the boards. Posts from the site’s seeded sample accounts are made up and are labeled “Sample”. See the terms of use.
